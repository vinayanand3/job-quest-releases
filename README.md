# Job Quest

A private, local-first Mac app for your job search. Keep one profile, tailor a resume and cover letter for every job with an AI model that runs on your own computer, and track each application from first look to offer.

This repository holds only the installers. Download the latest version from **[Releases](https://github.com/vinayanand3/job-quest-releases/releases/latest)**.

## Install

1. Download **Job-Quest-x.y.z-arm64.dmg** from the latest release.
2. Open it and drag **Job Quest** into **Applications**.
3. Open Job Quest. It's signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app.

New versions download in the background and install the next time you restart Job Quest.

## What you need

- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). 16 GB of memory is comfortable.
- [LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai) (free) with a chat model downloaded. Job Quest is built and tested with Gemma 4 E4B. The app's first-run setup and its **Help** tab walk you through installing LM Studio, downloading the model and starting its server.
- Optional: a free API key from JSearch or Adzuna for keyword job search. The **Help** tab explains where to get one.

## Privacy

Your profile, resumes, applications and notes stay on your Mac. The AI runs in LM Studio on your Mac, so your resume is never sent to an AI company. Job Quest goes online only when you use a feature that needs it (reading a job link, checking job boards, searching with your own key) and to check for updates, which you can turn off in Settings.
